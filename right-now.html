Right now

Or tap the day chart. Green is when you’re available; the dark block is the time you picked.
Your account’s farming ticks are shifted by a fixed number of minutes. One tree ready time is enough to work it out exactly, so the “Plant now” buttons give the right ready times. Leave it empty to assume no shift.

For each contract it plays out the next 4 days 300 times, once taking it and once swapping it for an easier one, with your schedule, lateness and patch care. Later contracts assume you ask for an easier one whenever it would take longer than a bush (2 h 40). Used and cleared preplants have to regrow. Clearing a growing plant uses a spade on the patch.
